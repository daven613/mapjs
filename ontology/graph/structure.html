Torah Map — Data Structure מבנה הנתונים

Everything in the map is one of three files: a concept registry, occurrence lists (the proofs), and a compiled graph (nodes + edges) deterministically rebuilt from the first two. This page shows each record type field-by-field, how a raw Hebrew phrase becomes (or fails to become) a graph edge, and where the good/bad distinction lives.

1 · The pipeline — how text becomes graph

Your hand extraction

legacy_human.jsonl

4,148 curated occurrences — each a relation you extracted by hand with a verbatim proof quote. Imported as occ:legacy:N.

AI extraction daemon (v1+v2)

ai_extracted/*.json → ai_compiled.jsonl

3,880 chunk files over all of Likutey Moharan → 16,007 candidate edges, each with a verbatim proof. Imported as occ:ai:<chunk>:N.

↓
↓

Endpoint resolution — registry lookup

Each occurrence's Hebrew surface forms are matched against concepts_final.json (3,588 canonical concepts; identity = the gloss) through a strict 4-rung lexical ladder (§4). No fuzzy matching, ever.

↓

compile_graph.py → nodes.json + edges.json

9,018 nodes · 12,328 edges. Deterministic and re-runnable: fix the registry or the policy, recompile, nothing is lost — the occurrence files stay the source of truth.

2 · Node kinds — three, not one

c: concept

3,588

A canonical Likutey Moharan concept. The only kind that carries a gloss and participates in search/match. All meaning lives here.

Edge identity = (source, target, type, polarity, via)

Occurrences that agree on all five aggregate into one edge and stack weight. Consequence: if the text attests both "X brings Y" and "X damages Y", the graph keeps two parallel edges, each with its own proofs — nothing is discarded. Also a consequence: a polarity-inversion extraction error creates a new wrong edge rather than corrupting the right one, so cleanup is deletion, never repair.

4 · The resolution ladder — surface → concept, or stub

Deterministic and lexical only. No gloss-similarity, no embeddings — a previous incident (פגם האמונה substring-matching אמונה) is the corruption class this design forbids. At every rung, a form owned by two concepts is treated as unresolved rather than guessing a winner.

  1. exactNFC-normalize, strip niqqud + te'amim, maqaf→space, normalize geresh/gershayim → match against every concept's forms + canonical_he.
  2. articlesame, after stripping a leading ה from each word (התורה → תורה).
  3. skeletonplene/defective folding (drop י/ו) — accepted only if the skeleton maps to exactly ONE concept in the whole registry.
  4. skeleton + articlerungs 2 then 3 combined.
  5. no match → policy forkone endpoint resolved → the other becomes a p: stub (edge kept, half-connected). NEITHER resolved → edge not stored ("skipped island"; the chunk file still holds it, so a recompile after an alias pass recovers them).

The structural bottleneck: 6,089 of 8,389 AI-only edges (73%) have a stub endpoint

The extractor quotes phrases the way the text says them (קדשת ארץ ישראל, inflected and construct forms); the ladder only accepts near-exact lexical matches. So most new edges dangle off placeholder nodes. This is by design — a wrong guess corrupts, a stub just waits — but it means the planned verified alias pass (~982 recurring unresolved forms) is the single highest-leverage fix in the project.

5 · Where good and evil live — and where they don't

Stored today: on the edge

polarity × via per edge captures both attested harm shapes: a bad thing acting (כעס מזיק — harms/presence) and a good thing missing (פגם האמונה מביא חלאים — harms/absence) without any node having to be "both good and bad".

Not stored: node character

No field says כעס is a bad concept and אמונה a good one. A bad↔bad bechina (כעס ↔ חמץ) is indistinguishable from a good↔good one. Your "bad begets bad" intuition has no home in the schema.

Proposed: derived node character

Computable from the edge signature — sources of harms/presence ⇒ bad node; sources of harms/absence ⇒ good node; "mixed" nodes get flagged for review (genuine dual concepts or extraction errors). A recompute step, not a schema break.

6 · Standing design decisions you may want to revisit

  • Legacy eitza edges were all imported as builds/presence. Correct for the overwhelming majority (the hand layer was curated as the good-flow map), but any hand edge that was really a harm-flow is mislabeled by policy. A cheap backfill pass re-classifying from their proofs was spec'd and never run.
  • Bechina/equation edges are coerced to neutral/presence. "The lack of X parallels the lack of its bechinot" stays a query-time inference in tmap rather than stored data.
  • Concept identity = gloss. All the audit's slug/content-mismatch bugs are violations of this rule by the AI canonicalization — the rule itself is sound; the fixes are renames/merges in the registry followed by a recompile.
  • The graph is a build artifact. nodes.json/edges.json are never edited by hand; every fix goes into the registry or occurrence layer and flows through compile_graph.py. That's why aggressive cleanup is safe.
