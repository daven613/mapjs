### 1. Node surfaces (check 1)

### 2. Good/bad/neutral (check 2)
There is NO node-level valence anywhere — concepts_final.json has no such field. What exists is per-edge and inconsistent:
- hand records: `polarity: {is_good, is_bad}` — 10/69 marked is_bad=1 (all justified); is_good never used; at least 2 edges that should be bad are unmarked (occ:legacy:1868, and פשעים→מלכות הרשעה).
- AI records: `polarity: "neutral"|"builds"` — a different scheme entirely (edge direction, not moral valence). No "blocks" appears in Torah 1 even for יצר-הרע edges.
Recommendation: valence belongs on the CONCEPT (node), not the edge — exactly as Shmuel described (mitzva=good, aveirah/bad trait=bad, object like the liver=neutral). Add `valence: good|bad|neutral` to the registry concept schema; edge-level builds/blocks stays separate. Torah 1 seed list:
