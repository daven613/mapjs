# The Clear-Laws Catalog — single-edge, literal, news-ready

**Why this exists (Shmuel's calibration, 2026-07-10):** of everything produced in the
first pilot round, the piece that landed was ONE attested sentence — *anger damages
livelihood* (I:66) — read literally, with the news event as a live instance of it
(a fight → the strait locks → money lost). Everything multi-hop, hinged, or figurative
read as "making stuff up," even when every hop was verified. The lesson: **clarity =
one hop × literal endpoints × the event instantiates the law.** This catalog is the
inventory of laws that meet that bar. Mining basis: 590 eitza edges whose two endpoints
both carry everyday meanings (out of 12,328 edges); the entries below are the
hand-verified clearest, each re-attested via `tmap chain` on 2026-07-10.

## The laws (each = one attested edge)

**Descent laws (what damages what):**
1. **Anger destroys wealth.** — I:59, I:69 — "when he is overcome by his anger, he
   wrecks the wall of wealth — anger damages it" (וּמְקַלְקֵל הַחוֹמָה שֶׁל עֲשִׁירוּת).
   Sister edge: anger → losing money outright, I:68. And the reverse system in the same
   teaching: wealth is itself a wall that restrains anger (I:59) — a feedback loop, all
   one teaching.
2. **Anger chokes livelihood.** — I:66 (כִּי כַּעַס מַזִּיק לְפַרְנָסָה) — the Hormuz law.
3. **Strife brings famine.** — I:39 — "the machloket — the insults people hurl — through
   this comes famine" (בָּא עַל־יְדֵי־זֶה בְּחִינַת רָעָב). News-ready for any
   conflict-and-food-prices story.
4. **Poverty comes from lost understanding.** — I:106 — אֵין עֲנִיּוּת אֶלָּא מִן
   הַדַּעַת (the Talmudic line, quoted as the law).
5. **Eating brings sleep.** — I:60 — וְעַל יְדֵי הָאֲכִילָה בָּא עָלָיו שֵׁנָה — everyday
   and literal; good for health/productivity stories.
6. **Falsehood damages the eyes — physically and spiritually.** — I:51 — literal claim in
   the text, though the *application* to trust/media needs one interpretive step; use
   with care (see the deepfakes pilot's mixed reception).

**Ascent laws (what builds what):**
7. **More charity, more peace.** — I:179, I:57 — מַרְבֶּה צְדָקָה מַרְבֶּה שָׁלוֹם (one
   line, already famous, zero unpacking needed).
8. **Peace brings healing.** — I:14, I:56.
9. **All cures grow from faith.** — II:5 — "all remedies are through herbs, and they grow
   only through faith" — startling and concrete; strong for medicine/health stories.
10. **Truth leads to wealth.** — I:47 — the builds-counterpart of the falsehood laws.
11. **Humility → honor** — attested only via intermediate steps (NOT a direct edge —
    checked); do not present as one hop.

## How to use (the Hormuz pattern)
Take a news event → ask "what was lost / what was built?" → scan this catalog (or run
the miner for the outcome concept) → if one law fits LITERALLY, the interpretation is:
(1) the event in two sentences, (2) the law in one sentence with its quote, (3) one
do-today action from the law's own terms. If no law fits literally — don't publish a
stretch; that's what the study-mode chains are for.

## Worked exemplars of the because-formula (v1.3, 2026-07-10)

**Deepfakes** — article: afraid *because* images can lie. Map (all I:51): שקר מזיק
לעינים (falsehood damages the eyes) · שקר, שהוא בחינת דמעות (falsehood IS tears — the
salt water that blurs the eye) · falsehood removes השגחת השם (the watchful care). The
chapter itself joins fake-seeing, crying, and lost protection.

**Congressman absent** — article: gone 117 days *because* of depression. Map: עיקר
גלותה כשעצבות גוברים עליה (I:24) — sadness exiles presence. Effects side (I:57): when
faith in the sages is lacking, that itself is the sages' departure — the article's
"drawing speculation" is that faith draining; the loop from both sides.
